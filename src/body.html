Smudge needs a home

Smudge is a small creature that eats colors. It lives on your Home Screen, where it can see through your camera, feel you tilt the phone and keep its swatch book offline.

  1. Tap Share. In iOS 26 it’s inside the ••• menu.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open Smudge from your Home Screen.
